THE GAME

WOBLO is a classic boomer shooter with a 90s spirit, combining brutal combat, dynamic exploration of multi-level maps, and a nostalgic pixel-art style. Get ready for waves of enemies, epic boss fights, and a retro-futuristic atmosphere!

CORE FEATURES

RETRO 3D RENDERING

WOBLO3D’s proprietary engine with multi-level maps, stairs, ladders, diagonal walls and MORE! Everything rendered in real time with full gravity and collision physics.

BRUTAL COMBAT

Dynamic combat with intelligent enemies. Different enemy types with unique attacks, patrol behaviors, and an FOV system. Boss fights that require real strategy!

ARSENAL WEAPONS

Weapon and rune system - mystical earth spirits. Each weapon comes with its own animations and effects. Collect ammo and upgrade your loadout!

EPISODE SYSTEM

Play selected levels or entire episodes in the style of classic Doom. Difficulty progression, persistent state between levels, and epic finales.

ADVANCED AI

Enemies patrol, track the player, avoid obstacles, and work together. The FOV system lets you surprise them... ...or they might surprise you!

DYNAMIC ATMOSPHERE

Atmospheric lighting, rain, shader, and a skybox that reacts to camera movement. Each level has a unique atmosphere!
